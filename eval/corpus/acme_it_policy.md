# Acme Robotics — IT and Facilities Policy

## Accounts and access

New starters receive their accounts on the morning of day one, provisioned by
the IT desk in Lisbon. Access to production systems requires a separate
request approved by the engineering director, and is granted for a fixed
period of 90 days before it must be renewed.

Contractors receive accounts that expire automatically on the contract end
date recorded in the HR system. Extending a contract does not extend the
account; a new request is required.

## Software

Employees may install software from the approved catalogue without asking.
Anything outside the catalogue needs a security review, which the IT desk
completes within five working days. Open-source libraries used in shipped
products must be checked against the licence allow-list before merging.

Personal password managers are permitted. Storing company credentials in a
browser's built-in password store is not.

## Hardware faults

Report a fault to the IT desk by ticket, not by email or chat. Loan machines
are available for collection in Lisbon and Rotterdam, but not at the Porto
office, where a courier delivers a replacement within two working days.

Damage caused by negligence — liquid spills, drops without a case — may be
charged to the employee's department budget at the director's discretion.

## Meeting rooms

Rooms are booked through the calendar system. A room that stays empty for
ten minutes past the booking start releases automatically and can be claimed
by anyone. The two largest rooms in Lisbon, Tagus and Belém, may only be
booked by managers because of demand.

Video conference kit lives in every room. Faults with the kit should be
reported as an IT ticket, not a facilities ticket.

## Building access

Badges work between 07:00 and 20:00 on weekdays. Weekend access requires a
request submitted by Thursday, and the building alarm must be disarmed at the
panel by the lobby within 60 seconds of entry.

The Rotterdam research facility has restricted areas where phones may not be
taken past the door. Lockers are provided outside those areas.

## Data handling

Customer data must not leave company systems. Exporting to a personal drive,
a personal email address, or an unapproved cloud service is a disciplinary
matter. Anonymised aggregates may be shared externally with director sign-off.

Backups run nightly and are retained for 35 days. Restore requests are handled
by the IT desk within one working day.

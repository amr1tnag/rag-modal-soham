# Acme Robotics — Employee Handbook (2024 edition)

## About the company

Acme Robotics was founded in 1987 by Maria Chen and Dev Patel in a rented
garage in Rotterdam. The company built its first industrial arm, the AR-1, in
1991. Headquarters moved from Rotterdam to Lisbon in 2019, and the Rotterdam
site is now a research facility only.

Annual revenue for the 2023 financial year was 4.2 million euros, up from
3.6 million euros in 2022. The company employs 68 people across three sites.

## Working hours

Standard hours are 09:00 to 17:30, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour unpaid
lunch break. Core hours, during which every employee must be reachable, are
10:00 to 15:00. Outside core hours, staff may start as early as 07:00 or
finish as late as 20:00.

Overtime must be approved in advance by a line manager. Approved overtime is
compensated as time off in lieu at 1.5 times the hours worked, not as pay.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote
arrangements require director approval and are reviewed every six months.
Anyone working remotely must be present on site for the monthly all-hands,
held on the first Thursday of each month.

## Leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave, plus public
holidays in their country of residence. Leave does not carry over into the
next year, except that up to 5 days may be carried into January and must be
used before 31 March.

Sick leave is uncapped but requires a doctor's note after three consecutive
days. Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver and
4 weeks at full pay for the secondary caregiver.

## Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop on their first day. The standard allocation
is a 14-inch machine with 32 GB of memory. Engineers working on simulation
may request a 64 GB machine through their line manager.

Laptops are replaced every four years, or sooner if a hardware fault cannot
be economically repaired. Personal use of company laptops is permitted within
the acceptable use policy.

## Expenses

Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense being
incurred. Claims submitted later than that are rejected automatically by the
finance system and require a director's written override.

Train travel is reimbursed at standard class. Air travel requires prior
approval for any journey under 600 kilometres, because the company policy
prefers rail on shorter routes. Hotel spending is capped at 180 euros per
night in Lisbon and 220 euros per night elsewhere in Europe.

## Security

Two-factor authentication is mandatory on all company accounts. Passwords
must be at least 14 characters and are never rotated on a schedule — rotation
is required only after a suspected compromise.

Visitors must be signed in at reception and escorted at all times inside the
building. Badges are returned on exit.
